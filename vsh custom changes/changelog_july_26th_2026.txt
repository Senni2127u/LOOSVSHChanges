# Kilt In Action: An update for LOOS's Versus Saxton Hale server

 Hello everyone! This is Delfite speaking, and I've got quite the update for you today. I've been working on this update periodically over the last week and a half,
 and I think its finally gotten to a point where I'm pretty happy with it. I couldn't quite get around to everything I wanted, but I had to stop myself before I
 succumbed to scope creep from all the ideas I kept getting during development. This update will primarily focus on Demoman, but has a little something for everyone
 else too.


 As with any set of patch notes though, if something isn't listed, that means it wasn't changed. And as any good developer knows, I've left commentary
 under most of the changes I've made. Of course, if anything feels too strong or wonky, we can always tone it down over time.

 So, without anymore preamble, let's get into the changes:


## Demoman
### All grenade launchers
- +25% projectile speed
- +25 max health on wearer

"Nobody likes dying in one hit, and I'm pretty sure most people don't like being forced to bring a specific item just to survive a basic attack from Hale.
 I took that to heart, and gave all grenade launchers 25 more max health. This does come with the side-effect of making Eyelander demos more versatile,
 but that's sort of the point. Increased gameplay variety is the goal, and in all honesty, the overheal granted by the Half-Zatoichi has demonstrated
 far better sustain compared to the movement speed and max health granted by the Eyelander." -Delfite

### Grenade Launcher
- Mag size increased from 4 -> 6
- Reserve ammo increased from 16 -> 24
- 20% faster reload
- 15% faster fire rate

"While the Grenade Launcher is already decent on its own, the slower projectiles can make hitting Hale pretty challenging at times.
 To compensate for that, and give the other grenade launchers some competition (even if it isn't that much), I've given it more ammo so missing your shots
 isn't as penalizing. The fire rate and reload time are meant to keep the weapon feeling agile, since demoman spends a lot of his time reloading." -Delfite

### Iron Bomber
- Fuse time decreased from -30% -> -80%
- Blast radius penalty removed
- Blast radius increased by 35%
- Blast jump damage reduced by 30% on wearer
- Self damage push force increased by 20% (Grenades send you way further now)
- Damage reduced from 150 -> 120 (-20%)
- Reserve ammo increased from 16 -> 24
- Mag size remains at 4
- Grenades still have very little roll

"Since the Iron Bomber was already known to be a sort-of jumper weapon, I decided to make it better at that.
 The blast radius buff is mainly to account for the short fuse on the grenades so missing isn't as punishing,
 and the damage penalty is there to balance out all the upsides granted by the weapon." -Delfite

### Loch-n-Load
- Damage increased from 150 -> 180 (+20%)
- Fire rate increased by 20%
- Now benefits from the global 25% projectile speed increase
- Mag size remains at 3
- Reserve ammo remains at 16
- Grenades still shatter on surfaces

"It felt weird to see the Loch-n-Load's damage bonus against buildings not be switched to a regular damage bonus, so I did just that.
 The fire rate bonus was added to make the weapon fit its theme: Hit hard, and hit fast." -Delfite

### Loose Cannon
- Unchanged

"I had an idea for what to do with the coconut gun, but it conflicted with how the gun already works.
 Right now, it's great for annoying the crap out of Hale and stalling him out if you know how to use it.
 With that in mind, I decided to just leave it alone, at least for the time being." -Delfite

### Boots
- No longer requires a shield to benefit from the movement speed bonus
- Movement speed on wearer increased from 10% -> 15%
- However! This speed boost is reduced to 10% if you have both the Eyelander and any stickybomb launcher equipped, so choose wisely!
- Now mirrors the stats on the Gunboats, granting 60% blast jump resistance and fall damage immunity.

"As strong as these stats may seem on paper, confirming kills on demoknights really isn't hard as Hale. His charge breaks their shields instantly,
 and allows him to quickly escape their melee range should he use it wisely. The added movement speed is really only there to make chasing Hale easier
 for demomen who aren't using the Eyelander, since even they tend to struggle with keeping up due to Hale's absurd mobility, but excluding the Eyelander
 entirely didn't feel right to me, so I only nerfed it if you're running it with a stickybomb launcher. As for the fall damage immunity,
 it falls under the same principle: Confirming kills isn't hard, you just have to use your abilities wisely and know when and where to take fights as Hale." -Delfite

### B.A.S.E. Jumper
- +25 max health on wearer
- +60% blast jump resistance on wearer

"Nothing else I tried for the parachute really worked, so I just gave it the stats from the boots, minus the movement speed of course.
 Not my proudest piece of work, but I think it's better than giving it nothing at all." -Delfite


### All stickybomb launchers
- +20% projectile speed

"Stickies, at minimum charge, don't go very far. They should now go slightly farther." -Delfite

### Stickybomb Launcher
- Damage increased by 10%
- Blast radius increased by 20%
- Fire rate increased by 25%

"While it may seem like these changes are strong, you might be surprised to learn that the damage is now nearly on-par with the Scottish Resistance.
 The key differences I noticed between the SR and the stock launcher was that they're both good at trapping, but the SR allows you the option to split
 your traps and see them through solid objects, which makes keeping track of your traps a whole lot easier. Stock doesn't have those advantages, but its stickies do
 arm faster, which can be useful for jumping away if you're using the boots for the newly-added blast jump resistance." -Delfite

### Scottish Resistance
- Unchanged

"It's fine as it is currently. The weapon's upsides are counterbalanced by its downsides, both written and implied." -Delfite

### Quickiebomb Launcher
- Increased blast radius by 50%
- Reduced mag size penalty from -50% -> -25%
- Reduced max stickybombs from 8 -> 6

"The quickiebomb's fast arm time makes it good as an offensive tool. Increasing the blast radius further facilitates that offensive playstyle,
 since it makes it easier to hit Hale. Of course, it won't be so easy if Hale decides to actually use his Sweeping Charge, but that's why I only
 slightly nerfed its ability to make traps. Note: As long as you fully charge your bombs, the 6 you have will still deal 1k+ damage as a trap." -Delfite

### Chargin' Targe
- Blast resistance increased from +30% -> +40%
- Added 60% blast jump resistance

"Grenade jumping is fun, and this change lets you do it more. The increased blast resistance also lets you survive Saxton Punch
 with 3-5 health left if you're at full health, which I think is fair since Hale can usually follow up with a slam if he's smart enough.
 There's also the fact that, well, it's not the Tideturner! Which means reduced mobility overall compared to it." -Delfite


### Melees
Unfortunately, I couldn't get around to tweaking all of demo's melees. But, I did at least take a little time to buff the caber
while I was busy cleaning up the recharge script. So, here ya go Caberknights, my early christmas present to you!

### Escape Plan
- +40% movement speed while active
- Damage reduced from 195 -> 126 (-35%)
- Movement speed no longer increases as your health depletes

"The Escape Plan's speed boost was a bit of an issue. For starters, the only time it mattered
 was when you were near death. If you were so much as grazed before getting hit by Hale though,
 you would die before you would even get the chance to benefit from the speed boost. Furthermore,
 since the speed boost only got better as you were hurt, Mad Milk scouts could cancel out your
 speed boost unintentionally because of the healing. For the same reason, this also discouraged
 use of the Concheror. For these reasons, I've decided to make the speed boost normal instead
 of relying on the player's remaining health." -Delfite

"And yes, I'm aware the Equalizer has the same issue with its damage boost. I'll work on that
 item another time though. Suggestions as to what should be done with it are encouraged." -Delfite
